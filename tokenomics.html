经济模型Tokenomics

MarsX 经济模型MarsX Tokenomics

经济模型用于解释生态资源如何围绕建设、社区、RWA 储备、团队锁仓和流动性进行配置。具体参数可在代币发行与合约部署后继续精细化。

Tokenomics explains how ecosystem resources are allocated across development, community, RWA reserve, team lock and liquidity. Detailed parameters can be refined after token issuance and contract deployment.

生态建设Ecosystem · 40%

用于产品开发、官网、数据看板、任务系统、Passport 和生态工具建设。

For product development, website, dashboard, missions, Passport and ecosystem tools.

社区激励Community · 25%

用于任务奖励、活动激励、传播贡献、早期用户和社区增长。

For missions, campaigns, promotion, early users and community growth.

RWA 储备Reserve · 20%

用于承载与 RWA 分红叙事相关的储备、透明披露和生态拓展。

For reserves, disclosures and expansion related to the RWA reward narrative.

团队锁仓Team Lock · 10%

用于长期开发和运营团队激励，建议采用公开锁仓和线性释放机制。

For long-term team incentives, ideally with public lockup and linear release.

流动性Liquidity · 5%

用于初始流动性、交易体验和市场深度支持。

For initial liquidity, trading experience and market depth.

后续细化Next Refinement

部署前需要明确总量、税率、交易限制、分红规则、Vault 地址和权限管理。

Before deployment: supply, tax, trading limits, reward rules, Vault address and permissions should be finalized.
