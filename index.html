MarsX Colony 白皮书MarsX Colony Whitepaper

正式摘要Executive Summary

MarsX Colony 是建立在 BNB Smart Chain 上的火星殖民主题数字生态，围绕 SPCXB 股票 RWA 分红资产、Mars Passport 身份系统、社区任务机制与链上增长体系，构建一个以“资产叙事、身份沉淀、生态参与、透明分配”为核心的长期项目框架。

MarsX Colony is a Mars-colony themed digital ecosystem on BNB Smart Chain. It is built around the SPCXB stock RWA reward narrative, the Mars Passport identity system, community missions and on-chain growth mechanics, forming a long-term framework centered on asset narrative, identity accumulation, ecosystem participation and transparent distribution.

1. 项目愿景1. Vision

MarsX Colony 的目标不是单纯展示一个代币，而是将火星殖民、资产分红、社区协作与身份体系整合为一个具有持续表达力的 Web3 生态入口。项目希望通过清晰的数据表达和统一的视觉系统，让用户能够快速理解生态结构、参与方式与价值路径。

MarsX Colony is designed to be more than a token page. It combines a Mars-colony narrative, reward distribution, community collaboration and identity infrastructure into a coherent Web3 entry point. The project aims to help users quickly understand the ecosystem structure, participation model and value path through clear data and a unified presentation layer.

2. 核心组成2. Core Components

  • SPCXB RWA 叙事：围绕股票 RWA 分红资产建立可读、可传播、可持续的价值表达。
  • Mars Passport：记录钱包身份、持有状态、等级、徽章与殖民地成长轨迹。
  • 生态看板：展示市值、持有人、分红池、交易活跃度等关键指标。
  • 任务系统：通过任务、成就和社区参与强化用户的长期留存。
  • SPCXB RWA narrative: a readable, shareable and durable reward story centered on the stock RWA asset.
  • Mars Passport: records wallet identity, holdings, rank, badges and colony progression.
  • Market dashboard: displays market cap, holders, reward pool and trading activity.
  • Mission system: supports retention through tasks, achievements and community participation.

3. 生态逻辑3. Ecosystem Logic

用户通过连接 BSC 钱包进入生态，在页面上查看代币数据、阅读规则说明、参与任务并积累 Mars Passport 身份。随着持有关系、社区贡献和任务完成度的提升，用户将逐步进入更深层的生态场景，形成“进入—持有—参与—升级—沉淀”的循环。

Users enter the ecosystem by connecting a BSC wallet, reviewing token data, reading the rules and taking part in missions. As holdings, contribution and completion levels grow, users move deeper into the ecosystem, creating a loop of enter → hold → participate → upgrade → retain.

4. 数据与透明度4. Data & Transparency

项目页面将优先展示可验证的市场信息与链上数据，包括总市值、持有人数量、殖民地数量、分红池余额和连接钱包后的代币余额。所有展示指标都应尽可能与公开合约、链上信息或后端可验证接口保持一致，以提升用户理解效率与信任感。

The site prioritizes verifiable market and on-chain data, including market cap, holders, colony count, reward pool balance and token balance after wallet connection. All displayed metrics should align with public contracts, on-chain data or verifiable backend sources wherever possible to improve clarity and trust.

5. 代币经济角色5. Token Utility

SPCXB 在生态中承担的主要角色是承载 RWA 分红叙事、建立持有者的身份归属感，并作为生态权益与活动参与的表达载体。代币不只是展示对象，也应是用户进入生态、理解生态和参与生态的主要入口之一。

Within the ecosystem, SPCXB primarily serves as the carrier of the RWA reward narrative, a source of holder identity and a medium for ecosystem participation. The token is not only a display asset, but also one of the main gateways for entering, understanding and participating in the ecosystem.

6. 长期发展方向6. Long-term Direction

MarsX Colony 后续将围绕更完善的身份等级、任务体系、持有人权益展示、生态合作入口与多语言传播页面持续演进，逐步构建一个面向全球用户的火星殖民主题 Web3 品牌。官网将持续作为项目的统一入口，承载市场数据、叙事表达与文档说明。

MarsX Colony will continue to evolve around richer identity tiers, mission systems, holder experience, ecosystem partnerships and multilingual communication. Over time, it aims to become a global Mars-colony themed Web3 brand. The website will remain the unified entry point for market data, narrative expression and documentation.
