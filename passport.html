Mars Passport

火星殖民身份系统Mars Colony Identity System

Mars Passport 是 MarsX Colony 的身份层，用于表达用户的钱包身份、持有状态、任务成就、徽章和殖民等级。连接 BSC 钱包后可显示地址，填入代币合约后可读取余额。

Mars Passport is the identity layer for wallet status, holdings, missions, badges and colony rank. After connecting a BSC wallet, users can view their address; token balance can be read once the token contract is configured.
